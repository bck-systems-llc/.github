## How an engagement works

Fixed price. Four steps. No lock-in.

| | Step | What happens | You get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Discovery** | We map how the work happens today, from the tools and data to the people, and find where time and money leak. | Findings and a shortlist |
| 02 | **Scope and price** | Each opportunity gets a plan, a fixed price and a clear definition of done. You choose what's worth building. | A fixed-price proposal |
| 03 | **Build and ship** | Working software in production early, shipped in small pieces with demos you can click, not status reports. | Shipped software |
| 04 | **Handoff and support** | Documentation, training and a clean handover to your team. Ongoing support is available after launch. | A system your team can run |
